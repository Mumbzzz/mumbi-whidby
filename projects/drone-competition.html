U.S. Army Drone Design Competition

Systems integration and remote piloting for a biologically fabricated, largely biodegradable UAV, built as part of the inaugural U.S. Army HBCU/MI (Historically Black Colleges and Universities/Minority Institutions) Drone Design Competition.

Overview

This competition, held April 23–24, 2019 at Sun Bowl Stadium in El Paso, TX, brought together 11 student engineering teams from Historically Black Colleges and Universities and Minority Institutions. My team paired with a multidisciplinary biology team to build a UAV with an airframe that was approximately 80% biodegradable. My role was on the engineering side: taking the bio-based airframe developed by the biology team and integrating the hardware needed to actually fly it.

What I did

  • Integrated flight control hardware, sensors, and electronics into the bio-based airframe.
  • Conducted ground and flight testing to evaluate and troubleshoot flight stability and handling.
  • Remotely piloted the UAV to complete obstacle navigation tasks during the competition.

Results

Placed 4th overall out of 11 competing teams.
