About

I'm a 4th-year Ph.D. student in Mechanical Engineering (Design, Robotics, and Manufacturing) at UCLA, advised by Prof. Veronica J. Santos in the Biomechatronics Lab. My current work centers on tactile sensing, robotic manipulation, and haptic feedback for real-world robotic platforms.

Earlier in my career, my work spanned robotics and hardware more broadly, from integrating flight control hardware on a bio-based UAV to designing payload systems for a NASA mission concept team to evaluating robotics integration for Honeywell's fulfillment operations.

Projects

Research · In preparation

Tactile Sensor Skin For Robotic Fingertips

Designed, fabricated, and characterized a tactile sensor skin across core geometry, overmold thickness, and taxel placement, evaluated through benchtop testing and grasping trials with a teleoperated LEAP hand, funded by the Office of Naval Research.

Research · In development

VLA-Based Manipulation With Tactile Sensing

A robotic manipulation system built on a Vision-Language-Action model with a custom dual-modality tactile sensor, enabling the robot to infer and compare physical object properties across sequential grasps.

Research · In development

Wearable Haptic Feedback Device

A wearable haptic feedback prototype to restore tactile discrimination for users in protective or sensing-degraded environments, such as underwater settings.

Research · Completed

Closed-Loop Tactile Teleoperation

Evaluated glove-based hand-tracking hardware and demonstrated a closed-loop system teleoperating a LEAP hand fitted with tactile sensors, relaying feedback through haptic display rings, as part of an ONR-funded bimanual teleoperation project.

Research · Completed

U.S. Army Drone Design Competition

Integrated flight control hardware and sensors into a bio-based UAV airframe and conducted flight testing as remote pilot in the inaugural U.S. Army HBCU/MI Drone Design Competition. Read more →
